the findingThe levels calibrate; the deltas do not. Those ratios are stable to ±1–3%, so the proxy predicts the board’s level well. Round to round it explains almost none of the board’s movement — residual rms 4.0% of the error, range -9.0 to +5.9%, and mvpe inverts sign in both tracks (R12→R13 proxy +2.34%, board −3.17%; T2R1→T2R2 proxy +1.25%, board −7.79%). A local delta below ~4% of the error does not survive the trip and must not be used to choose a package — every improvement measured since 2026-08-08 has been smaller than it, dropout’s included. That is why T2R2’s +0.200 reads as the closest instrument had the sign right (interior Re-band k-fold, rel −2.6%; the AoA 15–20 proxy said +0.66%, the wrong way) rather than as a confirmed local→board law.
