the finding结论The levels calibrate; the deltas do not. Those ratios are stable to ±1–3%, so the proxy predicts the board’s level well. Round to round it explains almost none of the board’s movement — residual rms 4.0% of the error, range -9.0 to +5.9%, and mvpe inverts sign in both tracks (R12→R13 proxy +2.34%, board −3.17%; T2R1→T2R2 proxy +1.25%, board −7.79%). A local delta below ~4% of the error does not survive the trip and must not be used to choose a package — every improvement measured since 2026-08-08 has been smaller than it, dropout’s included. That is why T2R2’s +0.200 reads as the closest instrument had the sign right (interior Re-band k-fold, rel −2.6%; the AoA 15–20 proxy said +0.66%, the wrong way) rather than as a confirmed local→board law.水平可校准，增量不可。这些比值稳定在 ±1–3%，所以 proxy 能很好地预测 board 的水平。但逐轮看，它几乎解释不了 board 的变化——残差 rms 为误差的 4.0%，范围 -9.0 到 +5.9%，且 mvpe 在两个赛道都反号（R12→R13 proxy +2.34%，board −3.17%；T2R1→T2R2 proxy +1.25%，board −7.79%）。低于误差 ~4% 的本地增量无法完成到 board 的迁移，不得用于选包——2026-08-08 以来测到的每一项改进都小于它，dropout 也不例外。因此 T2R2 的 +0.200 只能读作最接近的仪器把符号判对了（内部 Re 段 k-fold，rel −2.6%；AoA 15–20 proxy 给的是 +0.66%，方向相反），而不是一条已确证的 local→board 定律。
