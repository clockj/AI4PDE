# -- Round 11 WP-D', the LOSS side of sigma rather than the architecture side.
# sigma's gradient comes almost entirely from l_var = mean((log sigma - log s_true)^2) at weight
# 0.3, against l_tke (the scored quantity, rel-L2 between TKE FIELDS) at 0.15. Those two weight
# pixels completely differently: log-MSE is uniform, so it spends most of its gradient on
# low-amplitude pixels, while TKE rel-L2 is energy-weighted and only cares about the wake. That
# is why `var 0.334` -- a mean squared LOG error, sigma typically off by 1.78x -- coexists with
# a TKE rel-L2 of only 0.47.
# Round 10 tripled --lam-t and saw TKE move by 0.0001, but it left --lam-v at 0.3, so the term
# it was adding to was still outvoted 2:1. Lowering lam_v is the experiment that was never run.
# Risk, and what to look for: lam_v is also what keeps sigma sane where the TKE loss has no
# gradient, and BOTH the band and compose() read sigma everywhere. Watch rel-L2 and sps, not
# just TKE.
# Milder version of the same trade -- two points on the loss axis beat one, and this is now
# the only axis left that the information-floor argument does not already close.
# Added mid-run: fno_ltke is doing exactly what it was meant to (training tke 0.4464 against the
# baseline's 0.4709 at epoch 30) but its FIELD loss is 8% worse (0.1034 vs 0.0953). The variance
# losses backprop into the shared trunk, so tripling l_tke reshapes the trunk away from the
# field -- and rel_l2 is the highest-weighted subscore. --detach-var blocks exactly that path:
# the head still READS the trunk, so the physical conditioning is kept, but the TKE loss can no
# longer pull it. Round 10 tested --detach-var alone (best rel-L2, worst TKE 0.5625) with lam_t
# at 0.15; the point of pairing it with lam_t 1.0 is that the head then optimizes the SCORED
# quantity with the field trunk protected, which is the combination neither arm tested.
# CANCELLED for the same reason: --detach-var protects the TRUNK, but the failure above is the
# HEAD overfitting, which detaching does nothing about -- and round 10 measured detaching as
# costing TKE outright (0.5625, the worst of that round).
