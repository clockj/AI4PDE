## 1. Backbones — what is actually reachable, and under what licence

| model | shape | licence | reachable? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DINOv2-S/14** | ViT, **dim 384 / depth 12 / 6 heads** | **Apache-2.0** | yes, 88 MB, no token |
| DINOv3-S/16 | ViT, dim 384 / depth 12 | custom Meta, gated | **HTTP 401** without an accepted licence |
| Poseidon-T/B/L (scOT) | SwinV2 U-Net, time-conditioned | **CC-BY-NC-4.0** | yes |
| MPP / DPOT | AViT / FNO-ViT hybrid | MIT-ish | not on the HF API under any searched name |

**DINOv2-S is dim 384 / 6 heads, which is `models_alt.ViT2d`'s dim and head count exactly.**
That makes the user's question — does an image foundation model transfer to 32x64 PIV — testable
as a **pure initialization A/B**: same depth, same width, same parameter count, same recipe, same
`--seed`, same batch order, and **identical inference cost**, because a transplant is an
initialization and nothing survives into the forward pass but numbers. DINOv3 is the same shape,
so if the mechanism pays it is a URL change plus the user's HF token — but its licence sits badly
with the open-source requirement, which is why the mechanism is being tested on the Apache-2.0
model first.

**Poseidon is deferred, and the reason is latency, not licence.** scOT is a single-frame,
time-conditioned operator: `u(0), t -> u(t)`. Our window is 20 frames in, 20 out, so deploying it
in its own shape costs **20 forward passes per window** — the exact shape round 4 rejected for
DPOT and round 16 re-measured on U-FNO (batch-1 conv cost, `time` −0.207). Using it as a trunk
initializer for a 62->40-channel Swin U-Net means vendoring scOT, discarding the time
conditioning and the channel embeddings, and re-verifying the whole inference path — days of
work for the part of the model least likely to transfer. **CC-BY-NC-4.0 is a decision for the
user**, since the competition awards prize money.

### `Track_1/unet/dinov2_init.py`

Transplants the DINOv2 trunk into `ViT2d` and **verifies itself before writing**. A silently
wrong mapping produces exactly what "pretraining does not transfer" looks like, so the file
builds a from-scratch reference DINOv2 block straight from the published equations, runs random
tokens through both, and refuses to write above 1e-4. Measured: **8.2e-6 (L8) / 2.1e-5 (L12)**
relative — float32 noise, and consistent with the known fused-vs-python `MultiheadAttention` gap.

What maps: every LayerNorm, attention (HF keeps q/k/v as three Linears; torch wants them
concatenated `[q; k; v]` into `in_proj_weight`, which is the layout it splits back out), and the
MLP. **LayerScale is folded** into the row scaling of each residual branch's output projection —
algebraically exact, and verified rather than asserted. Position embeddings are bicubic-resampled
from DINOv2's 37x37 patch grid onto our 8x16. The patch embed cannot map (3 RGB channels through
14x14 against our 62 through 4x4) and the heads are ours. So it is a transplant of the **trunk**,
which is the part a foundation model is for. Output is written in `train2.py --init`'s own format,
so no training code changed.

Reading r22a/1, r22b and r22c together separates **"deeper helps"** from **"pretrained helps"**;
either alone is confounded.

**Why the ViT capacity cell is worth a slot on its own.** Capacity is the second largest
containment mover on record (+0.99 board sps, w24 → w96) and it has only ever been walked inside
*one* family. The FNO ladder is closed at w96 L8 — w160 is better per model and identical at
ensemble size. ViT is the best cross-family member we have (+0.39 marginal full-split sps on
cmb3sim x3) and it sits at d384 L8 only because that is what the round-15 architecture sweep
happened to try. L12 is also DINOv2's own depth, which is why it doubles as r22c's control.

## 3. r22d — two FREE levers, opened by the ensemble becoming heterogeneous

No training, no latency, no archive size. Both are questions *only* because the ensemble stopped
being K seeds of one recipe. `Track_1/ens_stack_v9.py`, fitted on held-out twins with
grouped-trajectory CV.

**P1 — member weights.** `predict_fno` averages members with weight `n_members`, i.e. uniformly.
For exchangeable seeds uniform *is* optimal and there was never anything to fit. A ViT next to
three FNO seeds is not exchangeable: the members differ in accuracy **and** in error correlation,
and the optimal weight of a member that is individually worse but less correlated is not 1/K.
Fitted over the simplex (the composition divides by the ensemble's own temporal std, so weights
that do not sum to 1 rescale the fluctuation before `alpha` ever sees it). **The weight enters
exactly where `nm` does, so anything this finds ships for free.**

**P2 — the band from cross-family spread.** Round 6 rejected ensemble spread as a band signal:
it correlated 0.347 with per-element |error| after the per-element mean was removed, against
`sigma_head`'s 0.551. **That was measured on three width-24 seeds of one recipe, where spread is
seed noise.** Across families spread is disagreement between inductive biases — a different
quantity. Fitted as a *second* exponent, `base * r_sigma^p * r_spread^q`, so `q = 0` reproduces
the incumbent exactly. `base` is refit inside every fold at every `(p, q)` by deflating the
errors, because `|E| <= base * r^k` is `|E|/r^k <= base` — **fitting `base` once at `q = 0` makes
every `q != 0` lose by construction, which is how the band was wrongly declared closed in round
6 and nearly again in round 10.**

The band is the largest historical mover of containment (+1.410), and bounds feed no other
subscore, so this is a one-sided bet.

## 4. What has to be true for any of this to ship

Every non-FNO member costs a **whole invocation** (0.435 board ms = −0.030 `final`); four
cmb3sim seeds fuse into one. So a family member must clear ~**+0.12 board sps** to break even,
and local `sps` over-reads containment 2-12x on rms-cutting arms. **The gate is `ens_v9.py` with
the band refit per set, at ensemble size** — five arms have shrunk from "clears the floor" to
"inside it" at exactly that step. `Track_1/verify/diff_arch.py` before shipping any non-FNO
member; `dilres` is **not** vendored into `_fno.py` and would raise inside the evaluation
container, which is deliberate.
