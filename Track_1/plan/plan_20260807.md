## WP1 — Batch the ensemble into one forward pass
**Expect +0.08 · hours · risk: near zero · no retraining**

`submit_fno/_fno.py:predict_fno` runs `for m in models:` — eight sequential invocations. Board
time tracks invocation count, not local wall time (round 9: 8 members were *faster* locally than
the U-Net and 2.04 ms slower on the board).

- [ ] Stack the eight members along the batch dimension, or fuse them into a grouped/blocked FNO
      with a leading member axis. Members are identical in shape except `w48`, `m1220`, `w64` —
      group by architecture, so three invocations instead of eight.
- [ ] Assert outputs are **bit-identical** to the current loop before and after.
- [ ] Re-time with `submit_fno/validate_strict.py`.

**Accept if:** outputs bit-identical and local invocation count drops. Board confirmation comes
with the next submission — do not submit for this alone.

## WP2 — Decimation-phase augmentation
**Expect 3–8 % error (≈ +0.3 to +0.7) · days · risk: low**

`unet/model.py:load_trajectories` does `f["u"][:, ::SUB, ::SUB]` with `SUB = 2` on 64×128 raw
frames. Three of the four decimation phases are discarded. They are real flow samples, not
synthetic perturbation — which is why this is not the input-noise augmentation we falsified.

- [ ] Add `--phases` to `load_trajectories` / `train2.py`; default off so every existing number
      stays reproducible.
- [ ] Train 3 seeds of the baseline recipe (`width 24, modes 8×12, --lam-grad 1.0`) with all four
      phases, **held out** (not `--all-data`).
- [ ] Score with `compare_v9.py --aoa 15 20` against the 3 existing held-out width-24 seeds.
      Validation data stays phase (0,0) only.
- [ ] If it wins, re-check whether capacity is still harmful — 4× data may move that ceiling.
      Re-run width 48 with phases before concluding.

**Accept if:** ≥ +0.15 mean4 on the AoA 15–20 held-out proxy, replicated over 3 seeds
(σ ≈ 0.05, so 3 seeds resolves +0.15 comfortably).
**Watch for:** the airfoil mask shifts half a coarse cell between phases. If the model cannot
absorb that, it will show as a loss on phase (0,0) immediately.

## WP3 — Train for the regime the board tests
**Expect 3–10 % board error (≈ +0.3 to +0.9) · days · risk: low, but only measurable on the proxy**

Error is governed by angle of attack — 0° 0.057, 5° 0.044, 10° 0.073, 15° 0.103, 20° 0.105 — and
the board's 0.113 sits at or above our hardest single trajectory. Training weights all AoA
equally, so most of the gradient comes from regimes the board barely tests.

Cheapest first; stop at the first thing that works.

- [ ] (a) Sampler reweighting: oversample AoA 15/20 windows by 2× and 4×.
- [ ] (b) Per-window difficulty weighting in the loss (weight ∝ running per-window rel-L2).
- [ ] (c) A high-AoA specialist trained on AoA 10–20 only, blended with the generalist.

**Accept if:** ≥ +0.20 mean4 on the AoA 15–20 held-out proxy.
**Critical:** evaluate on the AoA 15–20 proxy *only*. The full split will call a real board gain
a regression — that is the split being 46 % away from the board, not the change being bad.
Report the full-split number too, so the trade is visible and deliberate.

## Not doing, and why

| | reason |
|---|---|
| Band form / conformal intervals | closed — three attacks, flat response; conformal optimizes coverage, SPS is coverage × `exp(−2h/σ)` |
| Rollout stabilization, pushforward, commutativity reg., DPOT noise injection | we emit 20 frames in one shot; there is no rollout |
| Sim pretraining as a programme | measured at +0.00; the benchmark's own Foil rows show it buying only 9 % |
| Data assimilation / sparse reconstruction (~290 abstracts) | we are given the full field |
| More capacity | monotonically harmful — revisit only if WP2 changes the data regime |
| Input-noise aug, `--chan-pow`, `ALPHA ≠ 0.75`, spread-adaptive band, fitted fluctuation gain, input-mean MVPE blending | measured nulls, see `CLAUDE.md` |

## Standing rules

* Nothing is decided on a number from an `--all-data` checkpoint. Three contamination artifacts
  in three rounds. All-data models supply weights, never evidence.
* Evaluate on the AoA 15–20 **held-out** proxy. σ ≈ 0.05 mean5; a local gain under ~0.4 is
  unproven against the board.
* One submission per day, 100 per phase. **Submitting is the user's call.**

## Open question, blocking nothing

Whether it is usable is a **rules question for the organizers**, not a modelling one. Ask before
anything depends on it; build nothing on it meanwhile.
