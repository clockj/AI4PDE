## 2. THE FINDING: `sps` did not move, and the reason is that the TAIL did not move

`sps` went +0.008 / +0.020 where the proxy said +0.361 and the k-fold said +0.100. Decompose
the per-sample factor `a_i = 0.5g(rel) + 0.3g(tke) + 0.2g(mvpe)`, `g(m) = 0.5/(0.5+m)`, at
board metrics: rel contributes **+0.000468**, mvpe **+0.000330**, tke **−0.000459** — net
Δa = +0.00034, which at a containment fraction of 41.33/70.61 = 0.585 is worth **≈ +0.020 sps
on its own.** So the whole board move is the `a_i` factor and **the per-element containment
term contributed ~0 or slightly negative.**

> **Sim cut the rms and left the error TAIL exactly where it was.** That is the precise mirror
> of round 15, where capacity × dropout made `rel_l2` **0.64 % worse** and `sps` **+1.013
> better**. Two arms, opposite decompositions, and `sps` is 0.250 of the weight sitting at
> 41.33 against a ceiling of `100·a` = 70.61 — **7.3 of the ~13 points still on the table,
> more than rel_l2, tke and mvpe combined.**

## 3. What this closes, and what it opens

**Closed.** Every post-hoc transform (round 18 §5–7), the band's *form* (five closures), member
count (§7: +0.038 accuracy against a −0.041 latency bill), MC dropout (converges to the
deterministic model from below, −0.019 at sixteen passes), and — for `sps` — every loss-shaped
regularizer tried so far. **Latency is closed in both directions**: at the refit slope
`board = 3.274 + 0.336·local`, a zero-latency package is worth only **+0.064** more than
today's, and 10 local ms costs 0.17. There is nothing left to win or lose there.

**Open, and this is the round.** `sps` responds to the error TAIL, and the only change that has
ever moved it on the board is **a large step in model class with regularization moved to
match**. Three jobs are running:

* **`r19a` — capacity × dropout, re-tuned jointly** (7188138). R15 was never "capacity": it
  moved width 24→96 **and** dropout 0.05/0.15→0.30/0.45 together. Round 17 then took w128, L12
  and modes (12,20) up **at do45** and found they generalize the same. The diagonal is
  untested: `w128 do60`, `w160 do60`, `w160 do45`. **This is the honest thing `--int8` bought**
  — not a fifth member (worth zero) but a bigger one: w128 L8 is 51 MB packed so four fit,
  w160 L8 is 79 MB so three fit, where fp16 allows two and one. Cost to beat: ~+0.07 `final`
  (three w160 members give back ~0.02 of the K-curve and w160 costs ~0.5 board ms).
* **`r19b` — the `tke` regression** (7188140). `l_sim` reads `model(...)[0]`, the FIELD head
  only, so sim never trains `head_logstd` directly; it trains the SHARED TRUNK, which round 10
  measured as inseparable from the variance head. Sim's mapped windows carry **2.28× real's
  transverse turbulence intensity**, so the σ head reads a trunk pulled toward a fluctuation
  distribution real does not have. Round 17 Q3d already measured the dose: `lam_sim`
  0.1/0.3/1.0 is **flat on sps** (44.27/44.30/44.32) while **TKE is anti-monotone in λ** — and
  we shipped 0.3. Arms: `lam_sim` 0.1, 0.05, and (0.3 with `lam_v` back up to 0.6) to separate
  "dose" from "head".
* **`r19c` — the band's FITTING SAMPLE, which five band closures never touched** (7188141).
  `base` is 81 920 per-element half-widths and `optimal_band` sets each to an order statistic
  of that element's own observed |E| — from **126 windows**. Every closure so far was about the
  band's *form* at a fixed 126-window sample. `kfre_w96L8do45sim_f0..f4` already exist, so
  pooling their out-of-fold predictions gives the same 74 trajectories at ~10× the windows for
  no training. Only the SHAPE is taken from them (a fold model saw 4/5 of the data and its
  errors are larger); one scalar restores the scale where the incumbent is fitted; `sigma_ref`
  and `p` carry over untouched. Round 10b's band work was worth **+0.132 `final`**, larger than
  anything the model has produced since.
