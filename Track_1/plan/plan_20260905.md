   makes an output depend on its batch position — which is the LAST, SMALLER batch getting a
   different kernel. Pad every batch to a fixed shape and re-test; try fp16 autocast the same
   way. At w96 L8 the 1×1 convs are ~70 % of the FLOPs and are exactly what tensor cores
   accelerate. If the compute part (≈2.2 of 4.15 board ms) halves: **≈ +0.08 on each board**,
   and it re-opens the capacity axis, which is latency-bound (§1).

   views and mapped sim are the classic fix for memorization and cost **zero latency**.

   their latency. A step ships only if accuracy − latency > 0 at 4 members.

   candidate needs.
