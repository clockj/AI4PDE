## The open question: `--point-mlp`

The one reproducible finding of round 10 is that this model had **linear 1×1 bottlenecks**, not
a capacity shortage. Two were found and fixed, additively:

| | params | Δ wfinal |
|---|---|---|
| `head_logstd`: linear probe on a 24-ch trunk → 128-wide MLP (`--var-mlp`) | 3 408 | +0.086 |
| `lift`: linear 42→24 compression of the whole input window (`--lift-mlp`) | 7 568 | +0.076 |
| both | 10 976 | **+0.164** |

`point` is the third and last instance: `nn.Conv2d(width, width, 1)`, the local half of each FNO
block, **linear, one per layer, four of them**. Implemented and verified (trains, round-trips
through `_fno.load`, fuses); two seeds queued. If it is additive like the other two it is worth
roughly another +0.08…+0.16 and closes most of the remaining gap to 80.4.

## Closed this round — do not retry

`--lam-t` (tripling the TKE weight moves TKE by 0.0001 and costs the field) · `--detach-var`
(best rel-L2, worst TKE 0.5625, no better than a trajectory-constant predictor) · `--lam-f 0`
(a variance-only network is *worse* than the shared trunk — the field task is a useful auxiliary
for the variance task) · post-hoc sigma blending (−0.311 out-of-sample, third such null) ·
ALPHA ≠ 0.75 (0.90 wins by 0.041–0.070, inside the 0.055 noise floor) · **WP2** decimation
phases (compute-matched −0.009; at 4× steps −0.369, overfits) · **WP3** AoA reweighting
(monotone down: boost 2 −0.106, boost 4 −0.129, specialist −0.360; and worse on the *full*
split too, so it is a cost and not a trade) · width 48 (above).

## Two lessons worth keeping

* **Oracles rank directions, they do not forecast gains.** `oracle_split_v9.py` priced a perfect
  σ field at **+7.71** and five arms extracted **+0.08** of it. The ranking was right — σ was
  the correct thing to chase and TKE is where both adopted fixes paid — but the magnitude was
  never available.
* **Everything that redistributed what the model already had failed**; only removing linear
  bottlenecks worked. Reweighting data by regime, reweighting losses, reallocating the trunk
  between objectives, adding correlated samples, recombining σ after the fact — eleven arms,
  all negative or null.
