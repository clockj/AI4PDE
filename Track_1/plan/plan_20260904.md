* **The U-FNO miss is NOT explained by any local instrument.** The interior Re-band k-fold
  reproduces the proxy (rel −2.14 % / sps +0.92 on bands 1–3) where the board said +1.9 % /
  +0.44. Ruled out locally: all-data members trained normally (field 0.0337 vs the twin's
  0.0336), fp16 packing perturbs U-FNO and FNO alike (2.07e-4 vs 2.06e-4), Track-1 and
  Track-2 paths agree in-sample (0.0498 both). **What the k-fold does show is that U-FNO's
  advantage is monotone in Reynolds number and REVERSES at the top of the range**: band 0
  −18.2 %, band 1 −3.8 %, band 2 −2.2 %, band 3 −0.4 %, **band 4 +1.3 % (worse)**. A hidden
  set weighted toward the top of the speed range — in range (φ = 0), where the real grid is
  sparse — is consistent with the board's +1.9 %, with the board's AoA-hard error level, and
  with a local conv branch losing what it wins at low Re. Not proven; recorded as the working
  hypothesis. **Consequence: no non-FNO family ships on local evidence**, and the D1
  diagnostic asks where U-FNO's advantage lives spatially (zero-set boundary vs wake).
