## 2. What was built today

### `Track_1/unet/distill_labels.py` — the teacher labels
Runs an ensemble over every training window and stores exactly what `_fno.predict_fno` averages
**before `compose`**: the member-averaged raw field `(N,20,32,64,2)` and the member-averaged sigma
`(N,1,32,64,2)`, both float16, both in **normalized** space (the space the student's own head
predicts in). Sigma is one map per window, not per frame — the var head emits `(B,2,H,W)` and
`predict_fno` broadcasts it over time, which is why the label set is only ~5 % larger than the field.

* Rows are in `model.window_index(trajs, stride)` order and the index itself is saved beside them;
  `train2.py --teacher` compares it element by element, so a stride or split mismatch fails loudly.
* **Held-out discipline.** The split is never typed: `[]` with `--all-data`, `model.VAL_TRAJ`
  otherwise — train2.py's own rule at its line 715. Every member's `config` is checked against it and
  the script refuses a teacher that trained on the student's validation trajectories. (All five twins
  carry `exclude=None, all_data=False`, i.e. VAL_TRAJ; `re_split.excluded()` is the k-fold
  instrument's 26-trajectory Re split and is **not** what these checkpoints were trained against.)
* Members run in fp32 with TF32 off. `--verify N` re-runs `_fno.predict_fno(..., alpha=0)` — which
  short-circuits compose — on the first N windows and requires the denormalized label to reproduce
  it. **Smoke, 5 windows: field max |diff| 4.7e-5 against an rms of 0.0909 and an fp16 quantum of
  4.4e-5, sigma 1.2e-5.** The labels *are* the inference path, to fp16 rounding.

### `Track_1/unet/train2.py` — `--teacher`, `--teacher-sim`, `--teacher-mix`
The smallest change that expresses the arm: the **target** is blended once,
`y ← w·y_true + (1−w)·y_teacher` with `w = --teacher-mix` (0.5), and the σ head's target becomes the
teacher's σ outright. Every residual-based term downstream — `--loss`, the coverage loss, `--lam-grad`,
`--lam-tgrad`, `--lam-p`, `--lam-tail` — then sees one blended target.

* **Why blend the target rather than the losses.** The shipping recipe is `--lam-f 0.0 --lam-cov 2.0`:
  the MSE field term is *off* and the redescending Welsch coverage loss carries the field, so weighting
  `lam_f` would have done nothing. For an MSE term the two formulations have the same gradient; for a
  redescending one, a single blended target is the better-posed of the two (one residual, one bend).
* **Sigma from the teacher.** The ensemble's σ is a far better calibrated target than one window's
  `std_t(y)`, and σ is what the band — hence sps containment — is built on. T2R14 lost 0.273 of sps to
  a worse-calibrated σ head this morning, so this is the term to get right.
* `--teacher-sim` blends the **sim** branch's target the same way: the teacher labels the mapped sim
  windows, so sim stops being a wrong-but-related distribution and becomes more of the teacher's own
  function on inputs no real window covers.
* **Guards:** refuses `--norm self`, `--subpix`, `--mixup`, `--aug-scale`, `--aug-noise`, `--aug-mask`
  and `--aoa-boost` (the labels describe the unaugmented window); refuses a manifest whose stride,
  phases, `all_data`, exclude list or sim settings differ from the run's; refuses a misaligned index.
* **The default path stays bit-identical**, and `rp_dist0` §0 proves it rather than asserting it: three
  epochs of the cmb3sim recipe with sim on, at one seed, from the committed `train2.py` and from the
  patched one, compared on both the printed losses and the saved weights. A `kd` column is appended to
  the epoch line (the teacher-vs-truth distance, a diagnostic never added to the loss); existing column
  names and positions are untouched, as `tail`, `aux` and `psim` were before it.
