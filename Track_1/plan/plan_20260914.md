## 4. Phase 1 — screen (budget ≤ 80 GPU-h)

**Split and metrics.** The held-out twins' split (derive lists via `re_split.excluded()` /
the twins' configs; never type them). Report the full split and the AoA 15–20 proxy at
`--alpha 0.60 --band-p 0.45` with a band refit per model or set from held-out errors; also a
cheap ALPHA sweep {0.4, 0.6, 0.8} per new family (compose may want a different point).

**Recipe, identical for every arm, including a fresh FNO control.** The shipping `cmb3sim`
recipe **minus sim** (sim is ≤ +0.02 and doubles the data pass; it returns in Phase 2):
`--loss mse --lam-f 0.0 --lam-cov 2.0 --cov-c 0.25 --lam-grad 1.0 --lam-tgrad 0.3 --lam-v 0.15
--lam-t 0.15 --alpha-train 0.0 --var-head --stride 5 --batch 32 --lr 2e-4 --seed 1`, one epoch
count E_s for all arms (chosen so the costliest arm ≤ 4 GPU-h), held out (no `--all-data`).
Control = `--arch fno --width 96 --n-layers 8 --modes-y 8 --modes-x 12 --dropout 0.45 --var-mlp
--lift-mlp` at E_s. New families: σ head with an MLP readout sharing the trunk, dropout 0.3.

**Latency.** Every candidate plus the FNO w96 L8 and ViT references, **interleaved in one
process**: batch 64 (T1-like) and batch 1 (T2-like), fp32 and fp16 autocast, A100 ms/window and
the ratio to the FNO reference. Params and fp16 archive MB.

**Ensemble.** `ens_v9.py` (band refit per set): base {FNO×3 twins + ViT×2 twins}; base + X;
X replacing the ViTs; X alone (and X×K once seeds exist).

**Projection.** ΔFinal T1 / T2 against R25 / T2R12 with `predict_board.py` (anchors `r25`,
`t2r12` once scribe adds them): halve the local accuracy delta (the proxy over-reads ~2×),
charge +1 invocation for a partner family, FLOPs via `LAT_LOC_*`. Record the formula used.

**Wave 1, revised 09-14 after scout's triage (`lit_backbones_20260914.md`), in GPU priority order.
Pure PyTorch 2.2.2, no new dependencies.**
0. FNO control (above).
1. `cnext_unet`: ConvNeXt U-Net (The Well). RealPDEBench Foil ranks U-Net first on every metric
   (0.0145 vs FNO 0.0206). Its local full-resolution bias is the least like FNO+ViT. Scored as a
   **third-family partner**, as a **ViT-pair replacement** and standalone. Target 0.6–1.9× FNO FLOPs.
2. `swin_unet`: SwinV2/scOT-style, trained from scratch. Scored mainly as a **ViT-pair
   replacement**: one Swin at ≈0.7–2.8× FNO against 4.9× per ViT. It pays if it keeps the pair's
   containment at a third of the FLOPs.
3. `simvp`: SimVPv2 gSTA, **slimmed to ≈1.7× FNO**. The stock OpenSTL config is 16.8× at 20 frames.
   It is the best of 16 models on WeatherBench at 32×64, 12→12.
4. `ffno`: scored **only as an FNO-member replacement** (same family, and the capacity axes are
   closed).
5. `unet_mod`: only as a second conv arm if a slot is free, shrunk to ≤2× FNO (11.6× at hidden 64).
6. ~~`stvit`~~: **dropped**, 17–49× FNO FLOPs, and the ViT family saturates at two.

Literature not to expect: under-predicted TKE appears in every MSE-trained architecture, so a new
backbone should move rel and containment, not TKE.

**Wave 2:**
* second arms (size or dropout) of wave-1 survivors;
* a Re/AoA-conditioning **oracle** arm, as a diagnostic: only if true (Re, AoA) removes real error
  is a legal in-window estimator worth building;
* Track 2: an offline readout-refit check (scout) gating an ALPaCA-style closed-form Bayesian last
  layer. Build it only if the refit removes > 3 % error.

Not viable per the literature: DPOT, a space-time ViT, ConvLSTM, Poseidon (non-commercial licence,
`transformers` dependency), Walrus (1.3B).

**Decision rules.** σ_seed = spread of the three FNO cmb3sim twins.
* drop: standalone and partner projections both below 0 by more than 1 σ_seed;
* replicate (seed 2) before any decision within 1 σ_seed of +0.05;
* **advance: projected ΔFinal T1 ≥ +0.10 (standalone or partner, after halving), replicated.**
