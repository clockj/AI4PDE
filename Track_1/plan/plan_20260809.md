## WP-B — Cheaper local compute, which is now worth real points.  **+0.04 to +0.08 · hours**

At 0.431 board ms per local ms, the fused path's own inefficiency is costing score.

- [ ] **Fix the lift in `StackedFNO`.** It currently does `x.repeat(1, K, 1, 1)` and a grouped
      conv — but the input is *identical* for every member, so a plain `Conv2d(42, K*width, 1)`
      with the members' weights concatenated is mathematically identical and avoids
      materializing a K×-wider input. Free, exact, and the single biggest waste in the fused
      path. (Later layers genuinely differ per member and must stay grouped.)
- [ ] Profile `predict()` again end to end. Round 6's profile is stale: `pred ± band` was
      0.390 ms and the band is now per-window and materialized at full size.
- [ ] Consider float16 for the forward pass. The scored quantity is a velocity field with
      rel-L2 ~0.11; fp16 round-off is ~1e-3 relative. Check it changes no subscore beyond 1e-4.
- [ ] Re-time with `validate_strict.py` and convert through the board-time model before
      believing any of it.

      candidate exponent. Fitting it once at p = 0 is what made round 6 conclude the band was
      closed, and it nearly made round 10 repeat the error.

## WP-D — σ, the only large lever left.  **+0.05 to +0.3 · days · genuinely uncertain**

`oracle_split_v9.py` prices a perfect σ field at **+7.71**; round 10 extracted ~0.15. What is
left is not an oversight — it is the difficulty of predicting a 20-frame amplitude envelope
20–40 frames ahead in a flow whose shedding period is 60–75 frames. Three untried angles,
all consistent with the boundary/trunk rule (capacity at the boundaries helps, in the trunk
hurts):

- [ ] **Wider variance head**: 128 → 256 hidden. Pure boundary capacity, ~10k params, 1 run.
- [ ] **Multi-depth variance head**: read `concat(h1, h2, h3, h4)` instead of the final trunk
      state only. The amplitude envelope is a multi-scale quantity and the last layer may have
      specialized to the field. Still a boundary readout — no trunk capacity added.
- [ ] **Longer context for σ only.** 20 input frames is under a third of a shedding cycle.
      `--ctx 60` was tested in round 4 and failed, but that was on **sim**, on the **field**,
      before the variance head mattered and before the boundary/trunk rule was known. Feed the
      extra frames to the variance head alone, so the field trunk is untouched.

Order them cheapest first and stop at the first that moves TKE on the held-out proxy by more
than 0.005 (the seed σ).

## Standing rules (unchanged, all earned)

* Nothing is decided on an `--all-data` number. Four contamination artifacts in five rounds.
* Read the recipe off `fno_grad.pth`'s stored `config`, never argparse defaults, and keep a
  no-flags guard arm first in every queue. Three arms were lost to this in round 10.

  comparing, or the baseline wins by construction.
* Never compare a local cost against a board gain. Convert through the board-time model.
* Evaluate on the AoA 15-20 held-out proxy — but it is not the authority on member count.
* One submission per day, 100 per phase. **Submitting is the user's call.**
* Record the predicted board `final` and all five subscores here *before* each submission.
  Round 10's prediction is why the weighting model could be falsified at all.
