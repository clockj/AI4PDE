### 11.1 The matched private-set read (09-19 18:30 EDT): sim KEPT at the held-out cells

Every twin and both AoA holdouts train with `--sim-exclude-held`, which `train2.py` itself calls the
**lower bound** on sim's value ("sim teaches the regime"). Under the rules pivot the other end is the
deployment case: memo §2.4 places the private set on the release's **18 twin-less sim conditions** (8 grid
holes + Re 15225 / 27975 × 5 angles) — CFD in `train_sim`, no PIV in `train_real` — and the all-data
members already train on those 18 through the freestream law and the nearest mask.

* **It cannot be read on the AoA holdout.** `simreal.load_sim_mapped` takes the FOV mask from a real
  trajectory at the *same angle*; with a whole angle in `real_exclude` there is none, which is why forge's
  82cf301 fix skips those sim trajectories. A novel *angle* has no CFD in the release either, so the
  exclude-held AoA rows stay the right read for that case.
* **It can be read on a held-out CELL.** `pz_p2simS1in` / `pz_p2simS2in` = `pz_p2simS1/S2` minus
  `--sim-exclude-held`, seed-matched: sim at the 8 `VAL_TRAJ` cells stays in the pool, placed by the law
  and the nearest-Re mask exactly as the 18 twin-less conditions are, while `real_exclude` still withholds
  the twins' statistics (same cache file, no leak). `jobs/pz_p2simin_waiter.sh` submits both **behind**
  `pz_p2simS3` and inside the 4-job cap; ≈ 13 GPU-h.
* **What it decides.** K1(simin) − K1(sim), two seeds. If sim *at the condition* is worth several times
  sim *in the regime* (+0.03–0.05 in distribution), the Decision-phase method gets a sim lever that the
  dev board cannot see — oversampling the 18 twin-less conditions, or re-opening the `lam_sim` dose on
  this read — and the map's law/nearest-mask fallback becomes worth improving. If it is a wash, sim's
  private-set value is the regime effect already priced in §11 and nothing changes.
* Budget 09-19 18:30: 63 GPU-h used of 200 · forge's slate ≈ 34 · these two ≈ 13 · four all-data
  members ≈ 26 if the rule passes → ≈ 136.
