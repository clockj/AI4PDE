## 2. The bets for 09-17 → 09-20, ranked

**A. Distillation of the shipping ensemble into one model** (forge; new arm, not on the closed list).
Mechanism: a student trained on teacher-labelled inputs (stride-1 real windows, sim mapped into real
space, jitter) reproduces the 6-member mean AND σ at 1 invocation and ~¼ of the FNO group's FLOPs. The
latency credit is a same-family FLOPs cut — the one regime where the board slope has been measured to
hold — so it does not share the failure mode of fp16/Swin/graph. Worth +0.2 (T1) / +0.27 (T2) if the
student loses < 0.05 of accuracy; the K-curve gives the ceiling of what it must recover.
Pre-registered ship rule: proxy `final` (constant latency, K=5 held-out teacher as reference) ≥ teacher
−0.06 AND interleaved b64 fp16 step ≤ 0.6× R25's stack.

**B. `StackedFFNO` dense-path efficiency** (forge; code). The fused ×4 stack is 1.27–1.30× its member
loop at b64 fp16 (fixed ~27 %, not per layer). At ≤ 1.0× the Track-1 F-FNO L8 package is ≈ +0.13
(T2R13's accuracy +0.095 transfers across boards; mvpe −0.16 is the known exception).

**C. The 09-18 reads.** Track 1: the F-FNO L8 twin on Track 1 (R25's ViTs + the four all-data L8

## 5. Risks, stated before the results

* The student is a within-family FLOPs cut, but the ViT pair's containment (+0.05 sps each) is diversity
  the student may not reproduce; the K=5 teacher reference prices exactly that.
* A student's σ head is a regression to the teacher's σ, not to the realised error; band refit on the
  student's own held-out errors is mandatory before any proxy number is read.
* Family-level proxy inversions (U-FNO, Swin) mean the F-FNO-on-Track-1 read can miss by ±0.1 in either
  direction; it is a learning slot.

## 6. The rules pivot (confirmed 09-17 on the site and the Codabench API; memo `lit_20260917.md` §2)

> "The top 10 teams on the … leaderboard are shortlisted. Organizers re-train each shortlisted team's method
> from scratch on the competition's GPU cluster to ensure reproducibility, then evaluate on a private test set
> with unseen parameter regimes; the resulting private-test scores decide the top 3." Site: "private test set
> (unseen parameters, novel angles-of-attack / Reynolds numbers)"; evaluation on "8× NVIDIA A800".
> "A shortlisted solution that cannot be reproduced through re-training … is disqualified." External data,
> new simulations and outside pretrained weights are prohibited.

What changes:
1. **The development `final` is a qualifier (top 10), not the prize metric.** The user must read our RANK on
   both boards; if we are inside the cut, the leader gap is secondary and the slots are learning slots.
2. **From-scratch reproducibility is a hard gate** → work item D: `REPRODUCE.md` + `reproduce.sh` (twins →
   all-data members → band fit → build, 11 trainings), dry-run on our cluster with fresh seeds so that the
   re-trained method's score spread is known. Scout is auditing it now.
3. **The private set is OOD in AoA and Re** → work item C: AoA-holdout screens (AoA 10 and AoA 20 held out,
   sim at that AoA excluded too) for the cmb3sim FNO, its no-sim variant and F-FNO L8; the gated mean blend
   (`BLEND_BETA`, wins out of distribution in `mean_blend_re.py`) and the family choice are re-decided on those
   reads. R14's "hidden set in range" holds for the public validation set only. Scout prepares the job scripts;
   forge submits after the distillation jobs.
4. **`time` is measured on A800s** for the private set; our 4060-eq constants need not carry. Latency levers
   keep their dev-board value only.
5. **σ-amplitude modelling is closed on physical grounds** (memo §3.4): the TKE target is a 20-sample variance,
   our 0.538 already beats the hindsight long-run variance (0.570), and a 20 % cut would beat a 40-frame
   hindsight variance (0.456). No TKE-targeted arm gets GPU time.
