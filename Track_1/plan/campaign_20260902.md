## A packaging constraint the round hit: 8 x w96 does not fit in 256 MB

A width-96 member is **57.0 MB** against width-24's 3.6 MB, so the eight-member package the
latency table just cleared would extract to **456 MB against a 256 MB cap** — and `build_zip.py`
copied checkpoints verbatim, so the round would have discovered this at build time.

`build_zip.py --fp16` and `build_t2.py --fp16` (new, wired to `RPDE_FP16=1` in
`build_round13.sh`, which passes it to BOTH tracks so they cannot diverge) store every floating
tensor of the state_dict as float16: **57.0 -> 28.5 MB**, eight members **228 MB**, under the
cap. `torch.load_state_dict` copies with a dtype cast, so the model is still built and run in
fp32 and `_fno.py` needs no change.

Measured cost on `fno_all_w96do30_s1`, 16 `val_unseen` windows: max abs **4.3e-4**, rel-L2 of
the difference **5.5e-4** against a model rel-L2 of 0.078. Those add in quadrature, so it is
**2e-6 of the error** — three orders below the seed sigma. Safe for THIS FNO because its
spectral weights are real tensors with a trailing (real, imag) axis; a genuinely complex
parameter would need `pack_ckpt_fp16.py`'s split instead. `validate_strict.py` re-scores the
built archive through the official `scoring.py`, so a real degradation cannot slip past.

set of the FOV mask and the airfoil body, which a small U-Net branch can -- is the reading the
two controls leave standing.

## Q7 — the byte budget, and why the archive is 212 MB

**An FNO is its spectral weights and nothing else.** Parameters by tensor group, `w96` member:

| group | params | share |
|---|---|---|
| **spectral `w1`/`w2`** | **14.156M** | **99.4 %** |
| pointwise 1×1 | 0.037M | 0.3 % |
| the two heads | 0.030M | 0.2 % |
| lift MLP | 0.018M | 0.1 % |
| GroupNorm | 0.005M | 0.0 % |

`spectral = 4 · width² · modes_y · modes_x · n_layers` (the 4 is two blocks — positive and
negative ky — times the real and imaginary parts). Everything else is 1×1 convolutions on a
24-to-128-channel latent and does not scale with the grid or the modes. So:

| member | params | fp32 | **fp16** | **int8** | max members / 256 MB (fp16 → int8) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| w24 FNO L4 (the old shipped one) | 0.91M | 3.6 | 1.8 | 0.9 | 139 → 278 |
| **w96 FNO L4 (SHIPPED, 81.019)** | 14.25M | 57.0 | **28.5** | **14.3** | **8 → 17** |
| w96 FNO **L8** | 28.44M | 113.8 | 56.9 | 28.6 | 4 → **8** |
| w128 FNO L4 | 25.30M | 101.2 | 50.6 | 25.4 | 5 → 10 |
| U-FNO w96 L4 | 15.58M | 62.3 | 31.2 | 17.0 | 8 → 15 |

The staged archive is 228.6 MB extracted / 212 MB zipped = 8 × 28.5 MB of fp16 spectral
weights. Deflate recovers only 7 %, because half-precision weights are close to
incompressible.

* **int8 on the spectral weights is FREE — halve the archive for ±0.05 % of error, which is
  noise.** One scale per output channel per tensor (output-channel granularity because the
  einsum contracts over the input axis, so a scale error is a per-channel gain error, not a
  mixing error). **This is the single most useful engineering result of the round**: it takes
  8 × w96 from 228.6 MB to 114.7, makes **eight L8 members** fit where four did, and makes any
  mixed package trivially affordable. *Measured as a weight perturbation; storing genuine int8
  + scales in the blob and de-quantizing in `_fno.load_any` is a small, unwritten change.*
* **Low-rank factorization FAILS, decisively and informatively.** Truncating one layer's
  spectral tensor from (width², n_modes) to rank 48 keeps 69.6 % of its energy and multiplies
  rel-L2 by **2.9**. The FNO's operator is genuinely mode-specific: there is no small set of
  shared channel-mixing matrices modulated across wavenumber. This does NOT contradict AFNO
  scoring well at 0.24M — AFNO *trains* under that constraint and finds a different solution;
  projecting a trained FNO onto it does not work. Rank 96 (= n_modes, i.e. no truncation) is
  exact, confirming the harness.
* **Fewer members** remains the free lever, priced by the ensemble curve.
