  `final`. Replicates `submission.predict()`'s timed section exactly (predict_fno into a

  in the evaluation container -- a family is vendored only once it has won its ensemble test.

## A packaging constraint the round hit: 8 x w96 does not fit in 256 MB

A width-96 member is **57.0 MB** against width-24's 3.6 MB, so the eight-member package the
latency table just cleared would extract to **456 MB against a 256 MB cap** — and `build_zip.py`
copied checkpoints verbatim, so the round would have discovered this at build time.

`build_zip.py --fp16` and `build_t2.py --fp16` (new, wired to `RPDE_FP16=1` in
`build_round13.sh`, which passes it to BOTH tracks so they cannot diverge) store every floating
tensor of the state_dict as float16: **57.0 -> 28.5 MB**, eight members **228 MB**, under the
cap. `torch.load_state_dict` copies with a dtype cast, so the model is still built and run in
fp32 and `_fno.py` needs no change.

Measured cost on `fno_all_w96do30_s1`, 16 `val_unseen` windows: max abs **4.3e-4**, rel-L2 of
the difference **5.5e-4** against a model rel-L2 of 0.078. Those add in quadrature, so it is
**2e-6 of the error** — three orders below the seed sigma. Safe for THIS FNO because its
spectral weights are real tensors with a trailing (real, imag) axis; a genuinely complex
parameter would need `pack_ckpt_fp16.py`'s split instead. `validate_strict.py` re-scores the
built archive through the official `scoring.py`, so a real degradation cannot slip past.

**U-FNO IS THE BIGGEST SINGLE-MODEL RESULT OF THE ROUND, by a wide margin.** Proxy `wfinal`
82.500 against the best FNO variant's 82.206 and the shipped w24's 81.892; full split 84.663

**And the two controls it needed are both negative, which is what makes it interpretable:**
* **DilResNet 81.980 / 84.235** — a stack of dilated 3x3 convolutions reaches the whole grid in
  one block and fits BETTER than the FNO in training (0.0299 against 0.0391), yet generalizes
  worse than every FNO at this operating point. **The Fourier parameterization is doing real
  work; it is not merely a cheap global receptive field.** Round 4's verdict survives its first
  honest control.
* **U-Net at width 96 is barely above the shipped width-24 FNO** (81.897 / 84.029) and has the
  worst held-out `rho` of any arm (0.586) despite the second-lowest training loss. Round 12
  shipped a U-Net as a diversity member; as a trunk it is not competitive.

So U-FNO's gain is **not** "a conv net was better" and **not** "more parameters": it is the
FNO's spectral operator PLUS a local branch, and the local branch alone loses. The mechanism
the arm was proposed for -- mode truncation is a low-pass and cannot represent the sharp zero
set of the FOV mask and the airfoil body, which a small U-Net branch can -- is the reading the
two controls leave standing.

**NOT YET SHIPPABLE.** It needs (a) seeds 2-3 and a 3-vs-3, (b) all-data members, (c) its
latency measured, and (d) `UFNO2d` VENDORED into `submit_fno/_fno.py` -- `models_alt.py` is not
in the archive, and `_fno.load_any`'s repo-import branch raises inside the evaluation
container by design.
