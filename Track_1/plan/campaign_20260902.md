## Harness changes made this round

* `Track_1/lat_r15.py` (new) — end-to-end ms/window for a member SET, priced into board ms and
  `final`. Replicates `submission.predict()`'s timed section exactly (predict_fno into a
  pre-zeroed output plus the sigma-scaled band arithmetic), measures `_pick_batch` the way the
  package does, and takes the median of N passes. `path*K` repeats a member for timing.
* `Track_1/kfold.py --bands` (new) — pool a SUBSET of folds while keeping the checkpoint lists
  full-length, and always print the per-fold table. Under `--split re` the folds are Reynolds
  bands and `--bands 1 2 3` is the round's decision number (interior unseen Re).
* `Track_1/unet/models_alt.py` — four new families (`AFNO2d`, `DilResNet`, `UFNO2d`, `ViT2d`)
  behind `build()`, all with `FNO2d`'s exact interface. `Track_1/unet/model.py`'s
  `UNetTwoHead` gains `--dropout` (Dropout2d on the two encoder maps; no parameters, so the
  submission's vendored copy still loads it). `train2.py --arch` gains the four choices.
  `_fno.load_any` grows a repo-import branch for them, which works locally and raises loudly
  in the evaluation container -- a family is vendored only once it has won its ensemble test.
* GPU smoke (job 7135503): all five families train 2 epochs and load back through
  `_fno.load_any` + `fuse` + `predict_fno` with finite output. Params: unet 4.27M, vit 14.66M,
  afno 0.24M, dilres 2.38M, ufno 15.57M (against the w96 FNO's 14.24M and w24's 0.91M).
