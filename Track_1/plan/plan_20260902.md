## 3. Where the evidence points, and the round's bet

Round 14 measured that **the shipped w24 underfits** (in-sample ρ 0.588 on eight memorized
trajectories; w96/400 ep reaches 0.945) and that **capacity WITH regularization** moves the
held-out number for the first time (`capA`, one seed each, AoA 15-20 proxy `wfinal`):

| arm | proxy wfinal | full split | held-out ρ | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| w24 do05 150 ep (shipped) | 81.848 | 83.904 | 0.609 | |
| w48 do15 400 ep | 81.909 | 84.078 | 0.613 | |
| w96 do15 400 ep | 81.886 | 84.128 | 0.604 | |
| **w96 do30 400 ep** | **82.092** | **84.248** | **0.620** | gain is almost all **sps** (+0.92) |

Dropout is monotone through 0.30 and the trend is not exhausted; `capC` trained w96 do45 and a
second w96 do30 seed but timed out before scoring them and before the third seed. The board has
now confirmed the *regularization* half of that story with real money (+0.20). The round's bet
is the other half: **bigger models, regularized harder, and different model families at that
operating point.** The user has asked for risk and for new architectures; this is where the
evidence says risk is cheapest.

Two things could kill it, and both get measured first:
* **Latency.** w96 is 16× w24 in 1×1-conv FLOPs but the w24 package is launch-bound, so the
  real cost is unknown. Measure `fuse()`d 3× and 8× w96 end to end (`validate_strict`-style,
  idle node) BEFORE training all-data members at scale. Price: 1 board ms ≈ −0.07 `final`,
  A100 ms × 1.85 = 4060-equivalent, `board ms ≈ 2.6 + 0.435·inv + 0.803·local`.
  If 8×w96 costs > 2 board ms over the w24 package, the package is 4×w96 (or mixed).
* **The ensemble test (ROADMAP 1.15).** Every per-model gain since round 10 has shrunk or
  reversed at ensemble size. w96 do30 needs its third seed and a 3-vs-3 against w24 do05 ×3
  on the proxy, the full split, AND the interior Re-band k-fold before it ships.
