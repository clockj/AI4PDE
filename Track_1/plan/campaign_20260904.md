Two new flags landed this round (`f35b95f`), both adding **no parameters** and inert in
`eval()`, so every checkpoint loads through `_fno.load_any` unchanged:

* **`--mode-drop P`** (`models_alt.py:SpectralConv2d.forward`) — per-sample Bernoulli(1−p)/(1−p)
  mask over the retained `(ky, kx)` block, shared by the `+ky` and `−ky` halves (conjugate
  views of one physical wavenumber). Verified: `p = 0` is **bit-identical** to the pre-edit
  forward (maxabsdiff **0.0** on both `models_alt.FNO2d` and `submit_fno/_fno.FNO2d`);
  `p = 0.2` in `train()` changes the output (maxabsdiff 1.76) with mean |field| preserved to
  **+3.4 %** over 32 draws; `eval()` with `p = 0.2` is bit-identical to the control (0.0e+00).
* **`--lam-tail L`** (`train2.py`, right after `l_field`) — CVaR at 10 %: per sample, the mean
  of the largest tenth of the per-element squared field errors in the same `cw`-weighted
  normalized units as `l_field`. Reported as the new `tail` column, appended after `sim` so
  the existing columns keep their names and positions. Raises `NotImplementedError` under
  `--loss rel`.

### Verdicts (plan §3 drop rule: beat the control on **sps** on BOTH splits)

* **`s3` — KEEP.** Not an arm: the third twin of the control, and its job was to measure the
  seed floor. It does (see above), and it is fractionally the best of the three seeds on both
  splits. Available as a member.
* **`wd05` — DROPPED, and cleanly.** Loses on sps on both splits (−0.07 proxy, −0.03 full),
  loses rel-L2, TKE and MVPE on both, and **fits the training set BETTER than the control**
  (0.04721 vs 0.04746). That is the drop rule's second clause hit exactly: more fit, less
  generalization. AdamW decay at 0.05 is not a regularizer here — with `--dropout 0.45`
  already zeroing 45 % of the channels, the model is not in a regime where shrinking weights
  buys anything, and 0.05 is mild enough that it mostly rescales the effective learning rate.
  **The `--wd` axis is closed at this operating point.**
* **`md20` — DROPPED BY THE LETTER OF THE RULE, and it is the most interesting row in the
  table.** Proxy sps **43.91 vs 43.79 (+0.12)**, which is larger than the entire three-seed
  range (0.00); full-split sps **50.01 vs 50.04 (−0.03)**, which sits inside it. So it fails
  "both splits" on a margin of 0.03 against a seed floor of 0.05.
  → It is nonetheless the **only arm that behaves like a regularizer**: training field +27.6 %

Plan §Q3 named the two branches before the job ran: *"If the k-fold's interior bands land near

| **interior Re-band k-fold, bands 1-3** | **−2.14 %** (z −6.86) | **+0.92** |

**IT IS THE SECOND BRANCH: THE K-FOLD REPRODUCES THE PROXY, NOT THE BOARD.** It lands
−2.14 % / +0.92 against the proxy's −3.0 % / +1.10 and the board's **+1.90 %** / +0.44 — two
thirds of the proxy's gain, and **the wrong sign against the board on the subscore that carries
0.578 of the weight**. By the k-fold's own decision rule (|z| >= 2 AND the delta clears the
board's ~2 % transfer resolution) it calls U-FNO a win on rel-L2 *and* on TKE. The board says
rel-L2 lost. **The miss is unexplained, and the instrument question is reopened.**

* **The k-fold is NOT confirmed as a cross-family gate.** What round 14 and round 15 actually
  established is that it transfers *inside the FNO family* — dropout and w96 x dropout both went
  k-fold -> board within ~0.3 pp. On a change of architecture family it fails the same way the
  AoA proxy did, only slightly less. **Neither local instrument can currently rank two
  architecture families**; that is now measured twice on the same candidate.
* **U-FNO, ViT, AFNO and the cross-family trio stay closed anyway** — but for a *different*
  reason than the plan anticipated. They are not closed because a better instrument rejected
  them; they are closed because **no local instrument we own predicts a cross-family board
  delta**, and U-FNO's own board round already priced the family at −0.45 (and, at matched
  latency, +0.07 — inside noise). Round 16's bet on staying inside the FNO family stands, and
  it now rests on evidence rather than on preference.
* **Band 0 is exactly the retired regime, and it is where U-FNO looks best**: −18.23 % rel-L2 in
  the low-Re extrapolation band against −0.41 % in band 3. It is what drags the all-five-band
  number to −7.77 %. Round 14's φ = 0 measurement says the hidden set contains no such window,
  so the five-band table is a curiosity and the interior-band table is the read. Note the
  gradient: U-FNO's advantage decays monotonically as the band moves *into* distribution
  (−3.79 / −2.16 / −0.41), which is what an out-of-distribution robustness effect looks like and
  is consistent with the board seeing none of it.
* **A caveat that limits how hard this can be pushed**: the k-fold pairs *one seed per fold* per
  family, so the −2.14 % carries a per-model seed component that the 3-vs-3 ensembles average
  down, and the board ran 3 U-FNO members against 8 FNO members (ensemble-size mismatch worth

  k-fold did not catch what the proxy missed"**, not "the k-fold is wrong by 4 pp".
